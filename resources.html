Frequently Asked Questions

Before you start a project

Can Tryon handle both products and installation?

Yes. Requirements can include product selection, supply coordination, installation, configuration, testing and support. The exact scope is confirmed in the proposal.

Can I request a site visit and bill of materials?

Send the site location, project type, approximate device count and preferred timeline through the enquiry form. A site assessment and BOQ can be discussed based on scope.

Do catalogue product names represent fixed manufacturer models?

No. Catalogue names are presentation labels. Manufacturer, exact model, specifications, warranty, availability and pricing are confirmed in the quotation.

Does the website enquiry form store my information?

This static website opens your email application with the enquiry details. It does not submit to a website database. A hosted form service or backend is needed for online submission.

Can Tryon support existing CCTV or network installations?

Support can be discussed for existing systems after reviewing equipment details, site conditions, access and the required service scope.
