Typical applications

Common use cases for this product category include:

  • Building escape routes
  • Suitable exit and circulation points
  • Commercial fire alarm installations

What to confirm before selection

The right configuration depends on the site and the connected system.

  • Panel and circuit compatibility
  • Device type and reset method
  • Location, mounting and visibility
  • Applicable local fire-safety rules and inspection needs

Installation and integration

A product should be selected as part of a complete system rather than in isolation.

  • Check the site layout, cable route, mounting position and power requirements.
  • Confirm compatibility with the existing recorder, panel, network or management platform.
  • Test the installed device and document the final configuration before handover.
